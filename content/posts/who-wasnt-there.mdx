And what if AI could help us become better humans?

People who exchange with me often have probably understood that I am quite passionate about AI. This did not start four years ago, in November 2022. You all know what started then.

It started long before. It got more intense in 2020 and 2021, while I was making my switch from my older studies: philosophy, French language and literature, University of Athens.

---

That is when I started participating in conferences like [SNF Nostos 2021](https://www.snfcc.org/en/event/snf-nostos-2021/), where I also won the hackathon they organised.

A conference full of thinking. How do we, can we... In short: we have this technology, how do we make the world better? How do we preserve our humanity, with all that goes with it?

All this in a period when we hadn't even finished with Covid. And still, some people were thinking of a better future.

The whole conference was really inspiring for me. I can say it shaped the way I see AI.

---

My only concern is the people who weren't there.

Metaphorically, of course. I don't mean absent from this conference.

*I mean not interested in being there.*
